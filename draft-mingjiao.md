

- *Sunni Islam*: Believes Imam Mahdi has not yet been born. His name will be Muhammad bin Abdullah, and he will be a direct descendant of the Prophet Muhammad through his daughter Fatimah. He will lead the Muslim community and pray alongside the turun (descended) Nabi Isa (Jesus).

- *Shia Islam (Twelvers)*: Believes the Mahdi is Muhammad al-Mahdi, the Twelfth Imam. He was born in the 9th century and entered a state of divine concealment (occultation). He remains hidden until God permits him to reemerge to fill the earth with justice.

---

Physical Descriptions and CharacteristicsAccording to traditional Hadith compilations cited by scholars like Abu Dawud and Al-Hakim:

- Facial Features: He is described as having a broad forehead (dahi lebar) and a prominent, high-bridged nose (hidung mancung).

- Rule: His leadership or caliphate is widely predicted to last for seven years, during which time wealth and prosperity will overflow and fairness will prevail.

- Emergence: Mainstream traditions note he will emerge from the East, and his coming will align with other major end-times events like the appearance of the Dajjal (the false messiah).
